# EV Watch Visual Direction

## World

Urban charging intelligence presented as a measured data atlas. The interface is a working dashboard first. The atlas language contributes only four things: a warm paper-and-ink light surface, a graphite night surface, coral warning marks, and thin measured rails that connect a value to its context.

## Type

Be Vietnam Pro carries Vietnamese and English product copy. IBM Plex Mono is reserved for timestamps, model values, labels and compact measurement readouts.

## Palette

- Light ground: warm paper `#f4f0e8`
- Light ink: deep blue-black `#152331`
- Dark ground: ink navy `#0b1020`
- Dark surface: `#121a2b`
- Warning: electric coral `#f26d5b`
- Data: ice blue `#80d9f2`
- Secondary signal: spring lime `#d8ed77`
- Structural line: neutral blue-grey or warm stone, depending on theme

## Composition

The first viewport pairs a direct problem statement with a live-looking analytical plate. The dashboard is the main work surface. Sections use wide editorial measures and hairline rails instead of a stack of identical cards. The map and saved figures are evidence surfaces, not decoration.

## Signature Move

An atlas rail marks the reading path from area and time selection to probability, trend and spatial context. It is a 1px structural line with small detents that become active at the selected state. On smaller screens it moves into the flow and never obscures content.

## Motion

One authored entrance moves the rail and visual plate into place. The map selection ring, chart point and probability meter respond to real selection changes. Hover motion stays on transform and opacity. Reduced motion disables decorative movement while preserving state changes.

## Responsive Rule

Desktop keeps the map and reading panel side by side. Tablet moves the reading panel beneath the map. Mobile turns the dashboard into a single reading column, keeps controls at least 44px tall, and puts the atlas rail between sections rather than beside them.
